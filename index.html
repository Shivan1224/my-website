I spent my early career as a Business Analyst at Deloitte, working across the full software development lifecycle and running proof-of-concepts between onshore and offshore teams. That taught me how to turn messy requirements into something people can build.

How my automations work

Most workflows follow the same path: something triggers it, AI does the thinking, logic decides what happens next, and the result lands where your team works.

TRIGGERNew lead fills a form
AIQualify and summarise the lead
LOGICIs the score high enough?
ACTIONUpdate the CRM or sheet
NOTIFYAlert the team on Slack or email
